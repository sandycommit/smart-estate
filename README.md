# Smart Estate – Real Estate CRM & ERP Platform

## 1. Project Title and Description

**Smart Estate** is a comprehensive Real Estate CRM and ERP platform designed to streamline property sales, customer relationship management, post-sales operations, and overall business management through centralized dashboards and role-based access control.

## 2. Project Overview

The platform provides an integrated business management solution with dedicated administrative dashboards and module-based workflows. It helps organizations manage real estate operations efficiently, improve team coordination, and maintain centralized business data.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Centralize real estate business operations.
- Simplify property sales and post-sales management.
- Implement role-based access and permissions.
- Improve operational efficiency through dedicated dashboards.
- Provide structured workflows for different business departments.
- Enable efficient management of customers, employees, and business activities.

## 4. Key Features

- Role-based authentication and authorization.
- Admin dashboards and management interfaces.
- CRM and ERP module-based workflows.
- Property sales management.
- Post-sales operation management.
- Centralized business management.
- Reusable UI components.
- API integration and backend communication.

## 5. Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js, TypeScript
